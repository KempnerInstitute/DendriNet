About#

The Dendritic Modeling Framework#

The goal of the Dendritic Modeling codebase is to define and explore dendritic neural network models, inspired by the biological and morphological characteristics of neurons. By simulating how excitatory and inhibitory synapses process signals within neurons, particularly through shunting mechanisms, we aim to better understand both biological neural computations and develop more powerful artificial neural networks.

Key Features#

  • Biologically-Inspired Architecture: Models that capture dendritic branching, excitation-inhibition balance, and shunting inhibition

  • Flexible Network Configurations: Customizable branch factors, synapse counts, and layer topologies

  • Multiple Learning Strategies: Including maximum likelihood estimation, branch-wise training (freeze branches), and two-step learning approaches

  • Visualization Tools: Comprehensive tools for visualizing dendritic weights, activations, and branch analysis

  • Experiment Framework: Structured environment for running, tracking, and analyzing experiments across various datasets and model configurations

Research Applications#

The framework facilitates research in several important areas:

  • Understanding the computational advantages of dendritic processing

  • Developing more parameter-efficient neural networks through sparse connectivity

  • Exploring the benefits of excitation-inhibition balance in artificial neural systems

  • Investigating biologically-plausible learning mechanisms

  • Creating neural models with improved robustness and generalization capabilities

This project represents an interdisciplinary effort bridging neuroscience and machine learning, with the goal of advancing both our theoretical understanding of neural computation and the practical capabilities of artificial neural networks.
