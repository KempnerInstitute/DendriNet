About
=====

The goal of the Dendritic Modeling codebase is to define dendritic neural network models, based on the biological and morphological models of how different kinds of excitatory and inhibitory inputs are being received and processed in the neuron. By adding the shunting mechanism, dendritic structures and different types of 
synaptic inputs in modulating excitatory signals, which arereminiscent of biological processes observed in dendritic computation, we aim to better understand how real neuronal networks work and use this information to design better networks based on dendritic neuronal units.
