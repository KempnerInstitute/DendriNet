Additional Resources
====================

This page provides additional resources for learning about dendritic computing, biological inspiration for neural networks, and related research topics.

Academic Papers
---------------

Fundamental Dendritic Computing Papers
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* London, M., & Häusser, M. (2005). `Dendritic computation <https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.neuro.28.061604.135703>`_. Annual Review of Neuroscience, 28, 503-532.

* Poirazi, P., Brannon, T., & Mel, B. W. (2003). `Pyramidal neuron as two-layer neural network <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0896-6273(03)00149-1>`_. Neuron, 37(6), 989-999.

* Mel, B. W. (1994). `Information processing in dendritic trees <https://doi.org/10.1162/neco.1994.6.6.1031>`_. Neural Computation, 6(6), 1031-1085.

* Koch, C., Poggio, T., & Torre, V. (1983). `Nonlinear interactions in a dendritic tree: localization, timing, and role in information processing <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.80.9.2799>`_. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 80(9), 2799-2802.

Dendritic Computing in Machine Learning
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Guerguiev, J., Lillicrap, T. P., & Richards, B. A. (2017). `Towards deep learning with segregated dendrites <https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.22901>`_. eLife, 6, e22901.

* Sacramento, J., Costa, R. P., Bengio, Y., & Senn, W. (2018). `Dendritic cortical microcircuits approximate the backpropagation algorithm <https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2018/hash/f6a8dd1c954c8506aadc764cc32b895b-Abstract.html>`_. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 31.

* Hussain, S., & Bowers, J. S. (2020). `Neural networks with structural similarity to dendritic branches can perform nonlinear computations <https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-61609-0_23>`_. International Conference on Artificial Neural Networks, 282-293.

* Uria, B., Kononowicz, T., Watters, N., Muldal, A., Kaplanis, C., Kramár, M., Schneider, T., & Lillicrap, T. P. (2022). `Neural networks with recurrent dendritic compartments <https://openreview.net/forum?id=1GNWBRiAo8Y>`_. International Conference on Learning Representations.

* Beniaguev, D., Segev, I., & London, M. (2021). `Single cortical neurons as deep artificial neural networks <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2021.07.002>`_. Neuron, 109(17), 2727-2739.

* Ramaswamy, S., Bono, J., Schmutz, A., & Markram, H. (2022). `Dendriticus: a synthetic neuron model with dendritic computation <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2022.03.021>`_. Neuron, 110(5), 1769-1786.

* Wang, Q., Burkhalter, A., Wu, W., & Yang, G. (2021). `Dendritic spikes enhance pattern recognition <https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0901-20.2021>`_. The Journal of Neuroscience, 41(9), 2119-2132.


Datasets for Benchmarking
-------------------------

Standard datasets often used in dendritic computing papers:

* `MNIST <http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/>`_ - Handwritten digit recognition

* `CIFAR-10/100 <https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/cifar.html>`_ - Object recognition

* `Fashion-MNIST <https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist>`_ - Clothing item recognition

Specialized datasets
---------------------

* `Neuromorphic-MNIST <https://www.garrickorchard.com/datasets/n-mnist>`_ - Spiking neuron version of MNIST

* `SHD <https://zenkelab.org/resources/spiking-heidelberg-datasets/>`_ - Spiking Heidelberg Digits dataset

* `Spikefinder <http://spikefinder.codeneuro.org/>`_ - Challenge connecting calcium imaging data and electrophysiology
