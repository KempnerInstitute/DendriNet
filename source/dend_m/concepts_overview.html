Here is a mathematical equation:

The membrane potential dynamics in the Hodgkin-Huxley model are described by the following equation:

\begin{gather*} C_m \frac{dV}{dt} = I_{ext} - \left[ g_K n^4 (V - V_K) + g_{Na} m^3 h (V - V_{Na}) + g_L (V - V_L) \right] \end{gather*}

where:

  • \(C_m\) is the membrane capacitance,

  • \(V\) is the membrane potential,

  • \(I_{ext}\) is the external applied current,

  • \(g_K\), \(g_{Na}\), and \(g_L\) are the conductances of potassium, sodium, and leak channels, respectively,

  • \(V_K\), \(V_{Na}\), and \(V_L\) are the reversal potentials for potassium, sodium, and leak channels,

  • \(n\), \(m\), and \(h\) are gating variables for potassium and sodium channels.
