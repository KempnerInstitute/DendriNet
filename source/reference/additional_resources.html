Additional Resources

This page provides additional resources for learning about dendritic computing, biological inspiration for neural networks, and related research topics.

Academic Papers

Fundamental Dendritic Computing Papers

  • London, M., & Häusser, M. (2005). Dendritic computation. Annual Review of Neuroscience, 28, 503-532.

  • Poirazi, P., Brannon, T., & Mel, B. W. (2003). Pyramidal neuron as two-layer neural network. Neuron, 37(6), 989-999.

  • Mel, B. W. (1994). Information processing in dendritic trees. Neural Computation, 6(6), 1031-1085.

  • Koch, C., Poggio, T., & Torre, V. (1983). Nonlinear interactions in a dendritic tree: localization, timing, and role in information processing. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 80(9), 2799-2802.

Dendritic Computing in Machine Learning

  • Guerguiev, J., Lillicrap, T. P., & Richards, B. A. (2017). Towards deep learning with segregated dendrites. eLife, 6, e22901.

  • Sacramento, J., Costa, R. P., Bengio, Y., & Senn, W. (2018). Dendritic cortical microcircuits approximate the backpropagation algorithm. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 31.

  • Hussain, S., & Bowers, J. S. (2020). Neural networks with structural similarity to dendritic branches can perform nonlinear computations. International Conference on Artificial Neural Networks, 282-293.

  • Uria, B., Kononowicz, T., Watters, N., Muldal, A., Kaplanis, C., Kramár, M., Schneider, T., & Lillicrap, T. P. (2022). Neural networks with recurrent dendritic compartments. International Conference on Learning Representations.

  • Beniaguev, D., Segev, I., & London, M. (2021). Single cortical neurons as deep artificial neural networks. Neuron, 109(17), 2727-2739.

  • Ramaswamy, S., Bono, J., Schmutz, A., & Markram, H. (2022). Dendriticus: a synthetic neuron model with dendritic computation. Neuron, 110(5), 1769-1786.

  • Wang, Q., Burkhalter, A., Wu, W., & Yang, G. (2021). Dendritic spikes enhance pattern recognition. The Journal of Neuroscience, 41(9), 2119-2132.

Datasets for Benchmarking

Standard datasets often used in dendritic computing papers:

  • MNIST - Handwritten digit recognition

  • CIFAR-10/100 - Object recognition

  • Fashion-MNIST - Clothing item recognition

Specialized datasets

  • Neuromorphic-MNIST - Spiking neuron version of MNIST

  • SHD - Spiking Heidelberg Digits dataset

  • Spikefinder - Challenge connecting calcium imaging data and electrophysiology
